# CraftPilot recipe catalog: sources and notices

`Data/Catalog/*.lua` is generated by CraftPilot's own tools (`tools/catalog`). It holds facts (ids, numbers, names) read from the sources below; no source file is copied into it. The exact inputs and their SHA-256 are listed in `SOURCES.json`.

| Source | What it gives | License |
|---|---|---|
| WoW Forever client tables, as published by [wago.tools](https://wago.tools) | recipes, skill colours, materials, products, tools, cooldowns, recipe items and their requirements, vendor prices, names, camp objects | Blizzard's game data; World of Warcraft is a trademark of Blizzard Entertainment |
| [CMaNGOS classic-db](https://github.com/cmangos/classic-db) | trainer skill and fees for recipes Classic taught at trainers; quests that teach a recipe or grant a specialization | GPL-3.0 |
| [QuestieDB](https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB) (Forever release package) | vendors, drops and quest rewards of recipe items; NPC names and places; quest prerequisites | GPL-3.0 |
| [AllTheThings](https://github.com/ATTWoWAddon/AllTheThings) (Forever database) | the recipes Forever's trainers teach, what isn't released yet, Merchant's Favor prices and standings, learn skill of new Forever recipes | MIT, notice below |

Until CraftPilot 0.2.3 the catalog was a snapshot of the GPL-3.0 project [Craft Ladder](https://github.com/saintmodx/craft-ladder); from 0.2.3 on CraftPilot builds it itself and bundles no Craft Ladder data.
